# Kody the Carpenter — Page Map

Interior colouring pages. Cover is separate.

Captions are locked unless a better 5 to 10 word line shows up in testing. Art notes are the brief for whoever draws the page.

---

## Front matter

### Cover (not a colouring page)

**Title:** Kody the Carpenter
**Subtitle:** A Colouring Book

Kody full-body, centred, hard hat, tool belt, hammer up, thumbs up or ta-da. White or light workshop floor. One or two boards behind him. Colour the hat yellow. Leave the rest simple so the hat pops.

Do not put "Meet Kody" on the cover. That line belongs inside.

### Page 0 — Dedication (optional, light colouring)

Tiny Kody helmet icon or a single hammer.
Caption: "For little builders."

If you want this book to start faster, drop page 0.

## Opening sequence (pages 1 to 11)

This is the draft, tightened. Same 11-page count. Cleaner jobs for three of the pages.

### Page 1 — Meet Kody

**Visual:** Kody standing tall, big grin, tool belt on, hard hat on, arms out like ta-da. No extra props. Full body. Lots of white around him.
**Caption:** "This is Kody. Kody loves to build!"
**Why:** Cover sold the character. This page lets the kid colour him big.
**Watch:** Do not crowd with a workshop yet. That is page 2.

### Page 2 — Kody's Workshop

**Visual:** Kody in a simple garage-workshop. One workbench. One pegboard with 4 or 5 tool *outlines* (empty shadows of hammer, saw, tape, screwdriver). One little stool. One window. That is all.
**Caption:** "This is where Kody builds."
**Why:** The empty pegboard is a colouring gift and a setup. Tools come off the wall in the next pages. The stool comes back as build 3.
**Watch:** No parked truck, no cluttered shelves, no extension cords.

### Page 3 — Hammer

**Visual:** Kody holding a claw hammer up proudly. Other hand thumbs-up or on his belt. One nail and a scrap of wood on the ground is enough.
**Caption:** "Kody's hammer. Bang, bang, bang!"
**Why:** First tool. Onomatopoeia. Kids will shout this page.
**Watch:** Claw hammer, not a sledge. Head stays rounded and friendly.

### Page 4 — Saw

**Visual:** Kody at a sawhorse with a handsaw and a wood plank. Two hands on the saw. Simple sawdust dots (5 to 8, not a cloud).
**Caption:** "Kody's saw cuts the wood."
**Why:** Handsaw only. This is the most "real tool" page. Keep it calm.
**Watch:** Blade has a few big teeth, not a realistic tooth count. No power saw silhouette in the background.

### Page 5 — Tape Measure

**Visual:** Kody stretching a tape measure across a board. Tape is a long rectangle with a few numbers (1, 2, 3). Tongue out or focused smile.
**Caption:** "Kody always measures first."
**Why:** This is the book's brain. Measure before you cut. It pays off on every Plan page later.
**Watch:** Do not write a full inch scale. Three numbers. Big.

### Page 6 — Screwdriver

**Visual:** Kody with a screwdriver in one hand and a screw on a board. One tool. Big turn-motion spirals optional (2 max).
**Caption:** "Turn, turn, turn. The screw goes in!"
**Why:** Draft put screwdriver and drill on one page. That crowded the picture and mixed a hand tool with a power tool. Screwdriver gets its own page now.
**Watch:** Flat-head or Phillips, pick one and stick with it for the whole book. Phillips reads clearer.

### Page 7 — Tool Belt (full lineup)

**Visual:** Close-up of the belt on Kody's waist. Pouches with hammer (or empty hammer loop), tape measure, screwdriver, pencil, small level. Kody's t-shirt hem and pants visible so it is still him, not a product shot.
**Caption:** "Everything Kody needs is on his belt."
**Why:** Collection page. Kids love "find all the tools." This is the one busy page in the intro. Earn it.
**Watch:** Still 5 tools, not 12. No wrench set, no pliers pile.

### Page 8 — Safety Glasses

**Visual:** Kody putting on safety glasses. Both hands at the temples. Big happy face. Glasses are simple ovals. Hard hat stays on.
**Caption:** "Safety glasses on. Now Kody can see safe!"
**Draft caption was:** "Safety glasses on — now Kody is ready!"
**Change:** Do not say "ready" yet. Ears and gloves are still coming. Save "ready" for page 11.
**Watch:** Glasses over the eyes, not on top of the hat. Clear lenses (white, colourable tint).

### Page 9 — Ear Protection + Drill

**Visual:** Kody in ear muffs, holding the cordless drill. Little sound marks around the drill (3 or 4). Glasses already on from page 8.
**Caption:** "Loud tools mean covered ears."
**Why:** Draft had ears next to "a noisy tool." Make that tool the drill. One page teaches both. Drill does not need its own extra page.
**Watch:** Ear muffs, not earbuds. Cute rounded cups. Drill is a chunky cordless with a visible battery, not a pneumatic gun.

### Page 10 — Gloves

**Visual:** Kody pulling on work gloves, or one glove on, one going on, thumbs up once both are on. Hard hat, glasses, ear muffs already on.
**Caption:** "Gloves on. Safety first!"
**Draft caption was:** "Hard hat and gloves — safety first!"
**Change:** Hat has been on since page 1. This page is gloves.
**Watch:** Gloves are simple. Four fingers plus thumb. No leather grain.

### Page 11 — Ready to Build

**Visual:** Kody fully geared (hat, glasses, ears, gloves, belt). Standing next to a neat stack of wood. Excited pose, arms wide or one fist pumped. This is the jump-cut into the projects.
**Caption:** "Kody is ready. Let's build something!"
**Why:** Transition. Do not start the birdhouse on this page. Let this page be the deep breath.
**Watch:** Wood stack is 4 to 6 boards. Not a lumberyard.

## Builds (pages 12 to 41)

Three pages each. Plan / Build / Done.
On Plan: hat + belt. Tape out.
On Build: full safety (glasses, ears if the tool is loud, gloves).
On Done: hat + glasses + belt. Present the thing.

## Close (pages 42 to 44)

### Page 42 — Clean Up

**Visual:** Kody sweeping. Tools back on the pegboard (now filled, opposite of page 2). Wood scraps in a small bin.
**Caption:** "Build, then clean up."

This is the adult value page. Keep it one line. Do not add "a good carpenter always..."

### Page 43 — Pegboard Full (optional if you need the page count)

If you need a 44th colouring page, this is a quiet find-the-tool page: the pegboard with every intro tool hanging in its outline.
**Caption:** "Every tool back in its place."

### Page 44 — What Will You Build?

**Visual:** Kody facing the reader, geared down to hat + belt, holding a blank board like a sign. Big empty rectangle on the board for the kid to draw their own build.
**Caption:** "Your turn. What will you build?"

This is the best last page. Do not skip it.
